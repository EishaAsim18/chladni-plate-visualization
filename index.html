Idealized physics: the plate mode is the Dirichlet eigenfunction sin(mπx)sin(nπy); its zero set (nodal lines) matches multivariable calculus for u when cos(ωt)≠0. Coordinates (x, y) map to world X and Z; u is along Y. This is a membrane-style teaching model, not stiff-plate theory (real metal plates differ in spectrum and mode shapes). Sand is a simplified drift toward that nodal network (inspired by experiments, not a full grain simulation); allow a few seconds after changing (m, n). Use Play demo tone for a steady built-in pitch (same frequency bands as Audio-driven) when you do not want the microphone. Look reference: tabletop-style demo. If Pause is on, grains stop moving. Noisy mic in Audio-driven mode smears (m, n)—use Hold, a preset, or a steady tone. Camera only orbits if you enable Slow camera orbit.
